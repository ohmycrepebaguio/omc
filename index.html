Questions

Quick answers before you order.

What is a sushi-roll style crêpe?

A thin crêpe rolled tightly with filling, then sliced into neat bite-sized pieces, like a sushi roll. No fish involved.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, cash on pickup, etc.

How far ahead should I order?

Order the day before.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Unfortunately, we do not consider cancellations.

About

Oh My Crepe puts a playful twist on a classic dessert. Every crêpe is made fresh to order, rolled like sushi, and sliced into bite-sized pieces that are easy to share.
